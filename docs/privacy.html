Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Redirectory is a browser extension that blocks sites you choose. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

What is stored, and where

Your block list, settings, focus-panel links, tasks, theme and usage counts (how many times you were redirected, turned back or continued) are stored with chrome.storage.local — on your device only. Nothing is sent to us. We don't run servers.

Network requests

  • Site icons. To show a site's real icon when one isn't bundled with the extension, Redirectory asks Google's public favicon service (www.google.com/s2/favicons) for it, falling back to DuckDuckGo's icon service and then the site itself. Only the site's domain name is included in that request. Icons are cached locally so each domain is asked for at most about once a month.
  • Focus panel. Apps you add to the focus panel (for example Notion or Todoist) load directly from those services, the same as opening them in a tab. Their own privacy policies apply.

What Redirectory never does

  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No account, sign-in or personal information.
  • No reading or collecting of your browsing history. URLs are checked against your list in the browser and discarded.
  • No selling or sharing of data — there's no data to share.

Permissions

Each permission and the reason for it is listed in the README. The code is open source, so you can check all of this yourself.

Deleting your data

Use “Reset everything” on the settings page, or remove the extension. Both delete everything Redirectory stored.
