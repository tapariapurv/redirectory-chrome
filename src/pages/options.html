Welcome to Redirectory

Pick the sites that pull you off course. Next time you open one, you’ll land on your task list instead — with a way through if you genuinely need it.

  1. Block a few sites or a quick list below.
  2. Choose what shows in your focus panel — Notion, Todoist, or built-in tasks.
  3. Pin Redirectory to your toolbar to block any site in one click.

Blocklist

Sites are blocked including all their subdomains. Add a path to block just part of a site, like reddit.com/r/all.

Paste as many as you like, separated by commas, spaces or new lines.

      Add a panel

      Sign in first. Embedded apps use your normal browser session, so log in to Notion, Todoist etc. in a regular tab once. Redirectory lets these apps load inside its own page only — it never changes how they behave anywhere else.

      Friction

      How hard it is to get past the block. A little friction goes a long way.

      Blocking
      Master switch. Prefer “Pause” in the toolbar popup — it turns itself back on.
      Pause before continuing
      The continue button unlocks after this countdown. It only counts while the tab is in view.
      Ask what it’s for
      Require a short reason before continuing. Naming it is often enough to drop it.
      Time limits on offer
      Choose up to five. The shortest is pre-selected — a nudge toward less. “Rest of today” unblocks the site until midnight.
      Close the door when time’s up
      When your allowed minutes run out, open tabs on that site go back to the block page.
